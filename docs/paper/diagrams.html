15. 5-Day Rolling Evaluation Protocol

Figure 15 — The experimental evaluation protocol used to assess system performance over rolling 5-day windows with T+1 outcome checks.

gantt title 5-Day Rolling Evaluation Protocol (Round 2) dateFormat YYYY-MM-DD axisFormat %b %d section Day 1 Log: AAPL, GOOGL, AMZN decisions :d1log, 2026-03-09, 1d Check T+1 outcomes :d1check, 2026-03-10, 1d section Day 2 Log: AAPL, GOOGL, AMZN decisions :d2log, 2026-03-10, 1d Check T+1 outcomes :d2check, 2026-03-11, 1d section Day 3 Log: AAPL, GOOGL, AMZN decisions :d3log, 2026-03-11, 1d Check T+1 outcomes :d3check, 2026-03-12, 1d section Day 4 Log: AAPL, GOOGL, AMZN decisions :d4log, 2026-03-12, 1d Check T+1 outcomes :d4check, 2026-03-13, 1d section Day 5 Log: AAPL, GOOGL, AMZN decisions :d5log, 2026-03-13, 1d Check T+1 outcomes :d5check, 2026-03-16, 1d
Protocol: For each of the 5 evaluation days, the system runs the full 12-node pipeline for each ticker (AAPL, GOOGL, AMZN). The next trading day, the T+1 closing price is fetched and compared against the decision to evaluate correctness. After each outcome check, lessons are generated and stored in the KB, creating progressively richer context for subsequent days.
